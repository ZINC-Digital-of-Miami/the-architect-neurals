- The Preska order is a **permission to produce**, not an unsealing. Five headlines said otherwise; all trace to one Substack post.
- "Preska rejected all of Maxwell's objections Aug 11–12" was our own framing and is imprecise — there is no separate Aug 12 order.
- Katie Phang is the plaintiff; her posts are a party's characterization, not an independent origin.
- **The denominator exists now, and the units still do not match.** DOJ's own words, ECF 19 at 2: "The EFTA required the Department to review **over six million documents**." The numerator, recited by Judge Engelmayer citing the Jan 30 letter: "between December 19, 2025 and January 30, 2026, DOJ published **nearly 3.5 million pages** of documents covered by the EFTA, plus more than 180,000 images and 2,000 videos" (*Maxwell*, ECF 869 at 9). **Documents is not pages.** Keep them unpaired — but the anchor can now say why, and a subtraction between them is not a fact.
