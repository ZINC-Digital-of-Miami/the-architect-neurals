The Gap — July 19 → August 9

The war and the count. On July 23 the House passed H.Con.Res. 89 (Jayapal), 214–208, its second formal call to end the Iran war, while the Senate discharge on S.J.Res. 180 failed 47–49 A. A week later the Gillibrand discharge (S.J.Res. 181 — “direct the removal of US Armed Forces from hostilities within or against Iran”) failed 49–50, Paul, Collins, and Murkowski crossing one way and Fetterman the other, and the Senate left for recess until September 14 A, senate.gov. The last announced US strikes on Iranian territory came July 29 — CENTCOM: “dozens of IRGC facilities” after the July 28 IRGC missile attacks A. In the same window the Pentagon moved Iran-war casualties out of “Operation Epic Fury” into a new “Overseas Operations” category and restarted the public count — displayed KIA dropped 18→14; 600+ wounded became “207 wounded since July 7” — with the press office calling the anomalies technical and declining to define the new category B, ABC/WaPo/NPR/NYT origins.

The Week — August 17–23
