Documented record · SEC-filed company statement

Trump Media’s SEC-filed August 10 release says Truth API launched August 1 as a paid business-to-business feed for low-latency access to publicly available Truth Social posts.

The company said more than ten customer agreements had been signed and described criticism of the product as factually inaccurate. The statement does not resolve any claim about comparative public delay or legal compliance.

Documented record · Civil complaint and company counterposition

American Oversight and Campaign for Accountability filed a September 17 civil complaint in D.D.C. 1:26-cv-03258 alleging that paid Truth API access gives subscribers earlier access to official government posts.

The plaintiffs seek a declaration and an injunction against official posts through accounts covered by Truth API. Filing the complaint establishes their allegations, not judicial findings. Trump Media’s earlier SEC-filed statement describes a licensed feed of public posts; it predates this suit. Later docket responses or rulings were not inspected for this entry.

Documented record · Senate request for investigation and testimony

Senator John Curtis asked Senate Judiciary leaders to investigate presidential-family business dealings and subpoena both Donald Trump Jr. and Hunter Biden. His letter is dated September 21 and was released September 22.

Curtis asks the same oversight standard for both parties, citing foreign gifts, business interests and possible expectations of favorable treatment. The letter requests testimony; it does not issue subpoenas or establish misconduct. Its stated purpose includes examining existing ethics and disclosure law and possible reforms.
