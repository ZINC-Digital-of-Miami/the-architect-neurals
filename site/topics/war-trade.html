Documented record · Official sanctions-list action

On September 4, 2026, Treasury’s OFAC added Golden Global Yatirim Bankasi Anonim Sirketi, Golden Global Varlik Kiralama Anonim Sirketi and Golden Global Portfoy Yonetimi Anonim Sirketi to its sanctions list under the Iran E.O. 13902 program.

The official entries distinguish the bank, leasing company and portfolio-management company. The map records the U.S. designation action; it does not turn a program label into a finding that Iran owns these companies. The entries link the latter two entities to the bank without specifying an ownership percentage in this notice.

Documented record · Official license and scheduled expiry

Treasury’s OFAC issued General License CC on September 4, 2026. It permits specified wind-down transactions involving the three named Golden Global entities and entities they own at least 50 percent of, individually or together, through 12:01 a.m. EDT on September 19, 2026.

Payments to blocked persons must enter a blocked, interest-bearing U.S. account. Paragraph (b) retains other prohibitions under E.O. 13902 unless separately authorized. The license is a limited exception to sanctions, not an unblocking order or a general permission to continue business. The expiry is a scheduled boundary, not an observed future event.
