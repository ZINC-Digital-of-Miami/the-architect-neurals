Evidence & context

Documented record · Certified annual financial disclosure

Trump’s certified annual disclosure for 2025 lists Ladder Capital Finance LLC’s TIHT mortgage at $5,000,001–$25,000,000 with a 2026 maturity, two Axos mortgages above $50 million each with 2032 maturities, and a Charles Schwab Bank pledged-asset line above $50 million incurred in 2025.

Part 8 also reports the Bryn Mawr Seven Springs and Professional Bank South Ocean mortgages paid off in July 2025, and Ladder Capital Finance I LLC’s 40 Wall Street mortgage paid off in June 2025. The two Ladder names are distinct. These are the filer’s reported liabilities, not live lender balances or proof of the exact legal obligor on each underlying instrument. OGE released the certified report on 30 June 2026; it does not establish status on 8 September 2026.

Documented record · Issuer-filed subsidiary list and disclosure

Ladder Capital Corp’s subsidiary exhibit filed with its 2025 Form 10-K lists both Ladder Capital Finance LLC and Ladder Capital Finance I LLC under lending and real-estate debt subsidiaries. Both exact names also appear in Trump’s 2025 liability disclosure.

This establishes a dated common corporate-group connection. The exhibit does not specify ownership percentages, direct-parent layers or subsequent assignments. The creditors remain separate legal entities and their mortgages have different reported statuses; common group membership does not merge their roles.

Documented record · Company filing: governance roles

ALT5’s August 29, 2025 filing distinguishes Eric Trump’s observer designation from Zachary Witkoff’s accepted chairman appointment. It also records WLF’s right to nominate two directors and certain observation rights.

These are separately described roles in Item 5.02. The filing does not appoint Eric as a director. It separately describes Zachary Folkman’s conditional director appointment. The earliest event on the cover is August 25; August 29 is the filing date, not a proven effective date for every role. Later changes require later records.
