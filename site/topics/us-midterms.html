Documented record · Official calendar

The FEC lists November 3, 2026 as the next regularly scheduled federal general election.

This is a scheduled date, not a result or outcome forecast. State-specific administrative deadlines require their own current official records.

Event or scheduled date: 2026-11-03 · Source review: 2026-09-08

Documented record · Official election results; correction C-017

The FEC’s official 2024 results record Trump with 77,302,580 votes (49.80%) and Harris with 75,017,613 (48.32%); the electoral-vote result was 312–226.

This corrects the preserved Chapter 10 statement that treats 2024 as a national popular-vote loss. Trump won a plurality; a share below 50% does not mean losing the popular vote. It does not predict the 2026 midterms.

Documented record · Federal Register notices and court-record correction
