**Method note.** CourtListener's `/api/rest/v4/docket-entries/` requires authentication; only `/search/` is open, it throttles at roughly five calls a minute, and when it throttles it returns an **empty body** that parses as no results. Retry once before concluding a docket is empty — that failure mode will otherwise manufacture absences. Query child documents with `type=rd` and `q=docket_id:<id> AND <term>`, url-encoded, and compare what you get against the docket's document count. `ca4.uscourts.gov/cal/internetcalSept152026.pdf` works — the month is **abbreviated**; the spelled-out form 404s.
